      Our Story

      About

      The Warsaw High Financial Times was founded and is managed by a society of economics students who believe economics and finance shouldn't be limited by jargon and prior knowledge. From starting as articles about markets, macroeconomics and financial news headlines that nobody around us seemed to fully understand, we built you a medium where complicated concepts and mechanisms get broken down into ideas any can follow.

      Our wish is to continue writing for all curious students looking to broaden their knowledge within economics, finance, and the world of business around them.

      Complicated concepts and mechanisms, broken down into ideas any can follow.

      How we publish

      New issues are published roughly every one to two weeks, aiming to capture the latest and most consequential economic developments worldwide, not limited exclusively to the Warsaw area. Every article credits its author and the sources behind it.

        For Contributors

        Style & Submission Guide

        All submissions should be authentic, communicating economic and financial concepts clearly, precisely. If plagiarism is detected, it will not be published.

        Tone

        Use plain, precise language. A technical term should be defined at first use if it is necessary to the piece. Write in a clear, direct voice, favoring clarity over complexity.

        Structure

        Open with a concrete example or development, then connect it to the broader concept it illustrates. Most pieces run three to six paragraphs; there is no minimum length requirement.

        Sourcing

        Every factual or statistical claim must be supported by a credible source: a link, dataset, book, or interview. Original analysis and opinion do not require sourcing. List sources in Editor Tools' References field, one per line.

        Images

        Optional. Any image must be one you have the right to use, such as an original photograph or a clearly licensed image (e.g. Pexels, Unsplash), as Editor Tools uploads it directly into the page.

        Submission process

        Submit a pitch, consisting of a topic and a brief statement of angle, through the . Once approved, the piece may be entered directly in Editor Tools or submitted to an editor for entry.
